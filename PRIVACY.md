# ProofLock privacy summary

ProofLock's first APK release is local-first.

- Commitment names, completion dates, and proof timestamps are stored on the device.
- Proof photos are captured through the camera and are not uploaded by this release.
- The app does not include advertising, analytics, accounts, or third-party tracking.
- Camera access is used only when the user taps **Capture today's proof**.
- Users can remove local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Future releases may add optional AI verification or accountability sharing. Those features will require separate consent and an updated privacy policy before they are enabled.
